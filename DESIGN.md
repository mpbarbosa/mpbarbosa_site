# Design Documentation - Marcelo Pereira Barbosa Portfolio

## Philosophy
The portfolio is designed with a **Technical Minimalist** aesthetic, emphasizing precision, hierarchy, and a "dark mode" interface that reflects high-end engineering and AI research environments.

## Visual Language
- **Grid-First Layout**: Utilizing a subtle 40px grid background to evoke computational frameworks.
- **Micro-interactions**: Smooth transitions using `motion/react` for staggered entrances and hover states.
- **Monochromatic Palette**: A deep charcoal and black base (`#141313`) with pure white accents for high contrast and focus.

## Typography
We use a pairing of two distinct families to create rhythm:
- **Primary (Sans):** `Inter` for body text and labels, ensuring high legibility and a modern feel.
- **Display (Serif):** `Montserrat` (used for headings and branding) with heavy letter-spacing (`tracking-[0.2em]`) to give it an architectural, premium feel.

## Color System
- **Background:** `#141313` (Deep Neutral)
- **Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Surface Variant:** `#C4C7C8` (Mid-tone grey for secondary text)
- **Accents:** Radial gradients and line connectors to guide the eye vertically.

## Key Components
1. **The "Diamond" Emblem**: A recurring geometric motif signifying "precision" and "core logic."
2. **Project Cards**: Border-based cards that react to hover by intensifying the border opacity, using SVG visualizations for "latent space" and "kernel maps."
3. **Connectors**: Vertical hair-thin lines that create a sense of flow between sections, mimicking a technical schematic.

## Assets
- **Background Texture:** A desaturated, atmospheric image representing digital structures and networking nodes, used behind the "Current Focus" section with an 80% background overlay for depth.
